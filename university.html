Quezon City University — General Information & Directory

Quezon City University
Nurturing Minds, Transforming Lives, Building the Nation
Quezon City, Philippines Est. 1988 Local University
Vision

Quezon City University as a premier people's university in the National Capital Region that produces competent, principled, and socially responsive graduates who drive sustainable development.

Mission

Quezon City University commits to provide quality, accessible, and relevant higher education through instruction, research, extension, and production that develop lifelong learners who contribute to nation-building and global community.

Shared Values
Integrity — Upholding honesty, transparency, and accountability in all actions.
Excellence — Pursuing the highest standards of quality in education and service.
Nationalism — Fostering love of country, Filipino identity, and civic responsibility.
Innovation — Embracing creativity and new ideas to address community challenges.
Service — Committing to meaningful and responsive engagement with society.
